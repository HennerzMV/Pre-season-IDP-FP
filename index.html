See a worked example (Technical — First touch)
Outcome goal: I want to improve my ability to receive the ball out of the air.
Stop: Letting the ball bounce when coming towards me.
Start: Getting to the ball first before any bounce.
Continue: Practising with different parts of the feet and body — standing still and on the move, from different directions and heights.
Weeks 1–4: I do not let the ball bounce once in my games now.
Weeks 5–16: I am controlling every ball out of the air and keeping it close to me when standing still.
Weeks 17+: I can now control the ball out of the air when on the move.
